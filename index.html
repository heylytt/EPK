About

Born to shine through every storm.

LYTT — born Olusegun Ige-Olumide — is a Nigerian Afro Soul artist now based in the Netherlands, carrying the weight and warmth of Lagos in every note he sings. His music doesn't ask for your attention. It earns it, quietly and completely.

By day, Olusegun works in tech. By night and in the early hours before the world wakes up, he is LYTT — an artist who has turned personal struggle into sonic testimony. Songs like Falling, Rain, Let Go, and Your Life are not just records. They are the sound of a man refusing to let circumstance define him.

Rooted in Afro Soul with threads of R&B, gospel undertone, and raw emotional honesty, LYTT's music occupies the rare space between vulnerability and resolve. He doesn't sing about what he's conquered — he sings from the middle of the fight. That's what makes it land.

His debut EP, Rose & Promises, is a body of work built in the margins of a full life, and proof that passion held long enough eventually blooms.
